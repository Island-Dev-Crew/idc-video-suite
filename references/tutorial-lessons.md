# Tutorial lessons and limits

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HzXD4GVqXwM — reviewed from the timestamped transcript and sampled video frames on 2026-10-08. The third-party downloadable skill was not retrieved or copied. This is an original implementation of general workflow ideas, not an endorsement of all claims made in the video.

- Around 00:44–03:20: separate coordination, transcription, animation and evidence gathering. Here these are local seats with explicit outputs; a seat is a responsibility and does not require a separate autonomous agent.
- Around 09:42–11:15: improve style through a short first attempt and precise timestamp comments. Here feedback records identify the reviewed cut and time; accepted reusable rules compile into a durable, inspectable style file.
- Around 14:09–14:32: preserve the lesson from a correction for later sessions. Here unresolved and rejected suggestions remain in feedback rather than silently becoming rules.

Adoption choices: native Remotion remains the motion backend. Alternative renderers or paid generation tools can be selected per project after their actual availability and cost are established. The tutorial's music and fast scripted-retake approach do not override a documentary, voice-only brief. Structural checks, an inspected hook, full export checks and owner feedback answer different questions; none stands in for the others.
